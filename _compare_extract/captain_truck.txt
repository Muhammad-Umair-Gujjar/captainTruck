[Heading2] 1.1 Introduction
[] Road freight in Pakistan is large but highly fragmented: most operators own only a few trucks, and shippers still depend on informal brokers for load matching, pricing and cash settlement. Customers lack a single place to request a truck, compare routes, see a clear trip cost and track the vehicle live. Drivers lack a reliable assignment and navigation workflow. Administrators have no central panel for approve/reject decisions, driverâ€“truck assignment, live GPS oversight, chat moderation, cash confirmation and commission reconciliation.
[] Captain Truck is a comprehensive real-time logistics and dispatch platform with four surfaces: a Flutter Customer app (Android), a Flutter Driver app (Android), a React Admin web panel (Vite + Tailwind, hosted on Vercel), and a shared NestJS API (TypeScript) on an Oracle Cloud Always Free VM. Customers create dispatch requests with pickup/drop, truck type and count; the server offers 2â€“3 OSM route options with on-device AI trip-cost estimates; an administrator reviews, approves or rejects, and assigns a driver and truck; the trip is tracked over Socket.IO GPS; customer and driver chat (text, image, voice) with Smart Reply on mobile and 90-day auto-delete; payment is Cash-on-Pickup or Stripe Test Mode; and a commission ledger records what each driver owes the platform.
[] Data lives in PostgreSQL (Neon free tier), with Redis for live location and locks, Cloudinary for media, FCM for push, and OpenStreetMap services (Nominatim, Photon, OSRM) for maps and routing â€” all chosen to keep FYP cash spend near zero.
[] This chapter assesses whether the project is feasible, fixes its scope, estimates its cost and schedule, and introduces the team, tools, vision, risks and product features. Development follows the Incremental Model: after a planning phase, the system is delivered in four increments, each designed, built and tested before the next is added.

[Heading3] 1.2.2 Operational Feasibility
[] Customers need to request a truck, pick a route and cost, pay or choose cash, and watch the trip without calling a broker. Drivers need clear assignments, navigation-oriented GPS updates and a way to confirm cash. Administrators work in a browser (nothing to install) to approve/reject, assign, watch live trips, manage users/truck types/rates and confirm commissions. Unlike pure load-board apps that only match loads, Captain Truck keeps a human admin in the assignment loop â€” the core operational differentiator for trust and FYP scope control.
[] Verdict: Operationally feasible.
[Heading3] 1.2.3 Economic Feasibility
[] Function Point Analysis (Section 1.4.1) values the development effort at 26.09 person-months, or PKR 2,348,190 at market rates. For an FYP this labour is provided by the team, so actual spending is limited to a Google Play developer account, internet, electricity and printing. Cloud services are planned on free tiers (Neon, Upstash/Redis, Cloudinary, Vercel, Oracle Always Free, FCM, Stripe Test). On the benefit side, transparent route/cost options reduce broker opacity, admin mediation improves assignment quality, cash + commission ledgers cut reconciliation losses, and realtime tracking raises customer trust. Intangible benefits include industry-ready skills for the team and a demonstrable full-stack logistics product.
[] Verdict: Economically feasible.

[Heading3] 1.2.6 Information Feasibility
[] Pickup/drop coordinates and addresses come from the customer (with OSM geocode helpers). Route distance/duration/polylines come from OSRM. AI trip cost is computed on-device and validated server-side against distance sanity checks; the server bill (lockedBaseRate + aiTripCost, times truckCount) is authoritative. User, dispatch, trip, chat, payment and commission records sit in PostgreSQL with defined keys and constraints. Live GPS is held in Redis with periodic/last-known snapshots. Calculator and AI costs are labelled as estimates where appropriate.
[] Verdict: Information is available and reliable.

[] Table 1.15: Problem statement

[] fragmented truck dispatch, opaque pricing, weak live tracking and manual cash/commission settlement
[] affects
[] shippers (customers), small truck drivers and the administrators who coordinate them
[] the impact of which is
[] broker dependence, empty or poorly matched trips, unclear bills, lost cash reconciliation and low trust

[] Product position. For shippers and small fleet drivers in Pakistan who need trustworthy, admin-mediated dispatch with transparent cost and live tracking, Captain Truck is a real-time logistics platform that combines Customer/Driver Android apps, a React Admin panel and a NestJS API.
