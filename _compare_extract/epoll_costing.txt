We estimated cost with three main methods, Function Point Analysis, COCOMO II and
Activity Based Costing, and then cross-checked the effort with the LOC-oriented and FP-
oriented empirical models taught in SPM Lecture 6. All amounts are in Pakistani rupees (PKR)
and effort is in person-months (PM). Intermediate values are rounded to four decimal places,
effort and schedule to two, and each cost is worked out from the rounded effort and rounded to
the nearest rupee.
